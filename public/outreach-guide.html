KlivIQ Outreach: Quick Guide

Daily flow: Import → Audit → Review → Send → Follow up → Replies

1. Import

Add businesses to the list.

  1. Click Import.
  2. Google Places tab: pick a segment and a city, click Search.
  3. Check the preview, click Import.
  4. Have a spreadsheet instead? Open the CSV tab, upload or paste it, click Import.

2. Audit websites

Checks each website and ranks the ones that need a redo.

  1. Click Audit websites.
  2. Wait for it to finish. Stop pauses it.
  3. Open To send. The highest score is at the top.
  4. Modern sites move to Skipped automatically.

3. Review drafts

Every prospect gets a ready message.

  1. Click a business to open it.
  2. Read the message. Edit it if needed.
  3. Changed the wording? Click Regenerate all drafts at the top.

4. Send email

Sends from sriram@kliviq.com (daily limit shown at the top).

  1. Open To send.
  2. Click Send on a row.
  3. Confirm.

5. DM queue

WhatsApp, Instagram and LinkedIn messages, sent by hand.

  1. Open DM queue.
  2. Click Copy (English or Tamil).
  3. Click Open chat, paste, send.
  4. Back in the tool, click Mark sent.

6. Follow-up due

Reminds you who to nudge.

  1. Open Follow-up due.
  2. Click Send follow-up (email) or use the DM queue.

7. Replies

Finds replies, opt-outs and bounces by itself.

  1. Click Check replies.
  2. Open Replied.
  3. Answer from your Hostinger inbox.
  4. Not interested? Click Not interested.

8. Skipped

Sites that already look modern.

  1. Open Skipped.
  2. Want one anyway? Open it, click Contact anyway.

9. Other tabs

  • Sent: everything sent.
  • Closed: won, lost, do not contact.
  • All: everyone.

Only 10 to 15 emails a day. Never message anyone who said stop.
